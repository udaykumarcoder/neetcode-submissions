Why add these two cells?
To reach a cell, the last move must come either from the right or from below. So:
current cell = right neighbor + cell below

We move right-to-left, so the right neighbor has already been calculated.
Start: bottom row is all 1s. From any cell in the last row, there is only one way to reach the destination: move right.
