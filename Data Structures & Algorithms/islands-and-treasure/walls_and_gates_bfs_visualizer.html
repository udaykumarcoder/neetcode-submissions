Why this line?

Your INF doubt

The code does not need to mention the word INF. INF is just the starting value stored in empty land cells.

The addRoom guard rejects only out-of-bounds cells, visited cells, and walls (-1). So an unvisited INF cell passes the guard. It gets marked visited, added to the queue, and later overwritten with dist.

Why visited is enough: when a cell is enqueued, it is immediately added to visit. That prevents it from being enqueued again from another direction or another gate.
