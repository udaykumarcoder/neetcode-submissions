Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock with Cooldown · LeetCode 309

Simple example: prices = [1, 2, 3] · Expected profit = 2

Daily prices & current decision

At each day, choose cooldown or the action allowed by your state.
Maximum profit = 2
State:
buying=True → no stock held; you may buy.
buying=False → holding a stock; you may sell.
